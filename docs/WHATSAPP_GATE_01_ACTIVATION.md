# Gate 01 — WhatsApp Community activation pack

**Status:** Draft for RED MIST founder/moderator approval. **Community already exists**, with seven groups seen in an owner-supplied WhatsApp screenshot on 17 October 2026: the default Announcements and General plus SIGNAL, BUILD FLOOR, MISSIONS & TEAMS, HELP DESK, and BUILD TABLES / EVENTS. Logo/avatar and general community code appear set. Membership/permissions and pinned room messages are not verified or configured by this repository.

**Audience:** Current public beta 18+. Beginner-friendly. Cape Town-first, online participation can be expanded based on admin decision. Timezone Africa/Johannesburg (SAST).

## Mission and general standards

**Tagline:** Find your people. Build something real.

**Operating rhythm:** Commit. Build. Prove. Repeat.

All rooms inherit the [Community Charter](CHARTER.md): respect people; no harassment or scams; no personal data, secrets or private code in group chats; no unsolicited recruitment, advertising or DMs; agree project ownership, compensation and credit before work; communicate if blocked; evidence over unsupported claims; report safety incidents privately to a moderator. RED MIST registration and participation does not imply employment, funding or guaranteed team placement.

> 📢 RED MIST | ANNOUNCEMENTS
>
> This room is reserved for verified community notices: event schedules, community rule changes, registration windows and official decisions.
>
> 🔔 Check this room for changes. Ask follow-up questions in General or the relevant topic room.

**Moderator:** Community Lead or assigned Communications Lead. **Settings:** When available, set message sending to admins only. Publish no more than essential updates. **First post:** Announce the Community operating code and planned 14-day introductory cycle; dates only after confirmation.

### Room 1 — 🔴 SIGNAL

**Group description**

> Curated opportunities for RED MIST builders: hackathons, bursaries, competitions, grants, internships, events and credible industry updates. Include an official source, deadline, eligibility and whether fees apply. No spam or referral schemes.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 📡 RED MIST | SIGNAL
>
> SIGNAL is our opportunity radar—not an advertising channel.
>
> **Posting format:** OPPORTUNITY / WHO CAN APPLY / DEADLINE + TIMEZONE / COST / OFFICIAL LINK / WHY IT MATTERS.
>
> Verify the source before posting. Do not promise funding, placements or prizes. Discuss application help in HELP DESK; share personal wins in BUILD FLOOR.
>
> **First action:** Share one verified opportunity useful to a Cape Town or South African builder.

**Moderator:** Opportunities Curator. **Settings:** Curated posting by moderators initially; member suggestions privately or in General until processes mature.

### Room 2 — General

**Group description**

> RED MIST's welcome room. Introduce yourself, connect with builders, share relevant ideas, and ask where to start. Be respectful, protect privacy, and use topic rooms for technical support, mission recruitment and build progress. Current beta: 18+.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 👋 WELCOME TO RED MIST
>
> Find your people. Build something real.
>

**Moderator:** Welcome / Membership Lead. **Settings:** Members can message with moderation. Avoid requesting ID numbers, private phone numbers or sensitive details in chat. The complete Community Code is already pinned here according to the screenshot: keep it pinned and add this welcome note.

### Room 3 — BUILD FLOOR

**Group description**

> RED MIST's working floor. Make small commitments, show work in progress, share safe demos, request feedback and celebrate delivery. Monday COMMIT, Wednesday UNBLOCK, Friday PROVE. Share progress, not confidential data.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 🧱 RED MIST | BUILD FLOOR
>
> **MONDAY — COMMIT:** WHAT I'M BUILDING / DELIVERABLE / DEADLINE / HELP NEEDED.
>
> **WEDNESDAY — UNBLOCK:** WHAT I TRIED / EXACT BLOCKER / WHAT HELP WOULD UNBLOCK ME.
>
> **FRIDAY — PROVE:** PROMISED / COMPLETED / EVIDENCE OR DEMO / WHAT I LEARNED / NEXT STEP.
>
> Screenshots, accessible demos, learning reflections and documented plans count as effort. Don't upload confidential code, user/customer data or secrets.
>
> **First action:** Post a realistic seven-day deliverable. New learners can start with a simple webpage, a small design or an explained problem-solving task.

**Moderator:** Build Captain. **Settings:** Member posts allowed, one sticky routine, weekly prompt. Friendly feedback should be constructive and specific.

### Room 4 — MISSIONS & TEAMS

**Group description**

> Form real teams and complete scoped RED MIST missions. Founders and builders share project briefs, required roles, deliverables, dates, IP/credit and any compensation terms. No vague unpaid recruitment or guaranteed job claims.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 🚀 RED MIST | MISSIONS & TEAMS
>
> This room turns ideas into defined work.
>
> **Mission brief:** PROJECT / PROBLEM + INTENDED USER / 14-DAY OUTCOME / OWNER / OPEN ROLES / WORK EXPECTED / DEADLINE / PROOF OF SUCCESS / REMOTE OR IN-PERSON / IP + CREDIT + COMPENSATION TERMS.
>
> **Builder reply:** ROLE / EXPERIENCE LEVEL / AVAILABILITY / WHAT I CAN CONTRIBUTE / QUESTION.
>
> Share only project information you have permission to disclose. Collaboration does not automatically transfer ownership or entitle anyone to paid work.
>
> **First action:** Submit one scoped mission or indicate the role you are ready to contribute.

**Moderator:** Mission Lead. **Settings:** Admission by an appropriate moderator for active teams. Do not assume WhatsApp gives automatic access control based on website roles. Mission data and approvals belong in an access-controlled platform.

### Room 5 — HELP DESK

**Group description**

> Ask for help with coding, design, AI, product development, learning and entrepreneurship. Share what you're trying to do, what you tried, the blocker and a safe example. Help kindly; no mocking beginners or posting secrets.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 🛠️ RED MIST | HELP DESK
>
> **Ask for help using:** GOAL / TOOLS OR STACK / WHAT I TRIED / ERROR OR BLOCKER / WHAT ANSWER WOULD HELP.
>
> For coding questions, include a minimal reproducible example without passwords, API keys, private repositories or customer records.
>
> If you solve a problem, reply with the solution so others can learn.
>
> **First action:** Post a useful learning resource or one clearly described question. Beginners are welcome.

**Moderator:** Learning Facilitator. **Settings:** Member conversation enabled; prohibit credential-sharing and exploitative paid offers.

### Room 6 — BUILD TABLES / EVENTS

**Group description**

> RED MIST online build sessions, public-place co-working, demos, founder reviews and community events. Posts should include organiser, agenda, date, timezone, venue or meeting method, RSVP and accessibility details. Current beta: 18+.

**Pinned welcome message**

> 📍 RED MIST | BUILD TABLES / EVENTS
>
> We meet to build, not just network.
>
> **Event format:** TITLE / HOST / PURPOSE / DATE + SAST TIME / SAFE PUBLIC VENUE OR ONLINE LINK / CAPACITY / ACCESSIBILITY / RSVP / WHAT TO BRING.
>
> Expect a short intro, focused build session, peer support and a closing progress demo.
>
> In-person sessions need a named host and a suitable public venue. No attendee photos, personal contact lists or live location sharing without consent.
>
> **First action:** Vote on an online builder session and suggest a suitable public Cape Town meetup area. The exact venue and date will be confirmed by the organisers.

**Moderator:** Events & Safety Lead. **Settings:** Event hosts approved by admin; use RSVP privately if collecting personal information.

| Welcome / Membership | Application review, introductions and inclusive onboarding | Respond to all first-week introductions |

| Learning Facilitator | Troubleshooting, respectful peer support | Ensure questions are acknowledged |

One individual can temporarily hold several responsibilities, but the Community should have **at least two trusted moderators** before external recruitment, with separate incident-review/appeal coverage if feasible.

## First 14-day founder cohort

**Day 0:** Confirm policy, logo, admins, group settings. No public recruiting until safety and onboarding are ready.

**Day 1:** Announcements invites approved members to introduce themselves in General; Build Captain posts introductory goals.

**Days 2–4:** Help Desk supports first tasks; member introductions reviewed; qualified mission briefs submitted.

**Day 5:** Build Floor first demo day; celebrate progress, not member rankings.

**Day 7:** Gentle check-in for members who need support. Offer to adjust goals.

**Day 10:** Mid-cycle unblock clinic and mentor intervention when needed.

**Day 14:** First Odyssey review: who made progress, what evidence exists, what blocked delivery and what roles are missing. Share aggregate metrics, never confidential assessments.

## Website boundary and phase 2

Use the already-existing website access flow only after it passes security/privacy validation. Proposed journey: **website application → learner/builder/founder/mentor track → human review → adult-eligibility + informed WhatsApp opt-in → room invitation → first task → mission evidence → 14-day review**.

Potential pages `/community`, `/community/rules`, `/community/events` etc. are **proposals, not verified deployed URLs**. The chat invitation is intentionally absent here. Supabase records should be access-controlled with verified RLS, audit logs, consent retention rules and a member opt-out path. Never scrape WhatsApp participants or presume programmatic WhatsApp group management is deployed.
