# Community charter (DRAFT)

**Mission:** An accessible, accountable builders' network that helps adults turn learning, ideas and skills into tested products and companies.

## Participation and conduct rules

1. Take part: every active member chooses a realistic first 7-day task. Beginners welcome.
2. Respect everyone: no harassment, discrimination, threats, coercion, stalking or public shaming.
3. Represent skills, traction, project ownership and opportunities accurately.
4. Give context when asking for help: goal → what you tried → blocker → specific request.
5. Make and honour achievable commitments; communicate blockers promptly, and hand over responsibly.
6. No repetitive self-promotion, unsolicited sales, scams, affiliate spam or unsolicited direct-message recruiting.
7. Do not add people to other chats, publish phone numbers or photograph attendees without consent.
8. Never post passwords, confidential source code, customer data, private documents or addresses in public groups.
9. Agree project IP, credit, compensation and scope *before* substantive work; joining does not transfer ownership.
10. Meetings require clear venue, named host, public-place safety precautions and online option when practical.
11. Work only counts as delivered when agreed evidence and acceptance criteria are checked.
12. Report issues to moderators privately; comply with proportionate decisions and use the appeal route.

**Age:** Current public RED MIST beta is 18+. A younger student programme requires separate safeguarding arrangements.

**Moderation:** reminder → documented warning → temporary restriction or removal. Severe credible safety concerns may require immediate protection pending private review. A different reviewer should handle appeals where practical; never publish confidential reports.

**Membership:** Free beta. Hours are self-declared; meaningful progress matters more than available resources. At 14 days inactive, check in supportively; at 28, offer pause; at 42, move inactive participant out of active mission rooms with advance notice and re-entry option.

**Privacy:** collect minimal application data, separate WhatsApp opt-in, store consent decisions privately, limit admin access, publish privacy/retention contact, honour withdrawal and deletion requests. Do not collect SA ID numbers without specific legal/process need. Follow POPIA.

**Governance:** Community Lead, Membership Lead, Safety/Moderation Lead, Events Lead, Mission Leads and rotating Peer Reviewers. Conduct review after the first 14-day cohort.
