# Builder admissions and assessment (DRAFT)

We evaluate **intent, participation and ethical conduct**, not formal degrees or current coding ability.

## Tracks
- LEARN: aspiring developer/student/first-time builder.
- BUILD: engineer, designer, product, marketing, sales, operations, AI/data or maker.
- FOUND: founder with a problem to solve and roles to recruit.
- MENTOR: experienced volunteer whose claimed credentials and boundaries are separately checked.

## Application
Request preferred name, 18+ attestation, safe contact, chosen track/skills and level, a 14-day goal, a 7-day first task, realistic availability, example of problem-solving or follow-through, rules agreement, privacy notice acknowledgement, and **separate** WhatsApp number visibility/opt-in. Portfolio links are optional.

## 100-point rubric
| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Motivation and clarity of next 14-day goal | 20 |
| Realistic time/commitment and first task | 25 |
| Proof of effort (past artifact **or** beginner challenge) | 20 |
| Constructive communication/collaboration | 20 |
| Ethical conduct and reliability | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Suggested interpretation:** 70–100 active; 50–69 guided trial; under 50 explorer orientation and retry. Safety concerns trigger human review regardless of score. Require two reviewers for rejections, sensitive cases, and mentor privileges. Provide explanation and appeal/re-application route.

## First 7-day assessment task (60–120 minutes; accommodations allowed)
- New developer: simple page or a documented debugging exercise.
- Founder: customer problem, five interview questions and a realistic validation plan.
- Designer: user-flow sketch with one usability improvement.
- Growth/sales: customer profile, ethical outreach and measurement idea.
- Product/operations: five milestones and key dependencies.
- Research/AI: reproducible experiment and limitations.
- Without equipment: handwritten structured plan or guided oral explanation accepted.

**State:** APPLIED → REVIEW → ORIENTATION/TRIAL/APPROVED → CONSENTED INVITATION → FIRST TASK → ACTIVE / PAUSED. Never automatically invite to WhatsApp on form submit.
